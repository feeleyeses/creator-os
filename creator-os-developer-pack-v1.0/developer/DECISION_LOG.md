# Decision Log

## Locked decisions

### D-001 — Language is a first-class module
Language is a window into culture and the world, not a secondary study tool.

### D-002 — Today is the default entry
Default navigation order starts with Today.

### D-003 — Default state is Alive
The app opens with useful content immediately visible.

### D-004 — Today focuses on the present
The product prioritizes today's useful content over past/future timelines.

### D-005 — AI is secondary
AI appears as short editorial notes and expands only on request.

### D-006 — Quiet Editorial Design
Use clear hierarchy, warmth, reading comfort and restrained motion.

### D-007 — Quantitative and qualitative growth coexist
Future growth reviews may show numbers, new fields understood and new knowledge connections.

### D-008 — Build before over-specifying
Once worldview and principles are stable, implement the functional skeleton first and refine through use.

## Rejected

- Infinite scrolling
- Empty-by-default home
- Timeline-first growth home
- Forced completion flows
- Large AI-generated text blocks
- Streak-based pressure
- Excessive preview steps
