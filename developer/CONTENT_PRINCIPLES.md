# Content Principles

- Images exist to explain, not decorate.
- Every important claim should have a source.
- AI selects what is worth the user's time; it does not merely collect information.
- Less information, more understanding.
- Language is learned through real context.
- Language filters must never mix languages.
- Content should be useful before it is comprehensive.
- Build the functional content skeleton first; refine after real use.

Content should answer:

- What happened?
- Why does it matter?
- What should the user take away?
